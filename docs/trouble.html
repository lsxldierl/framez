When something looks wrong

What the When something looks wrong does, and what it is for.

A menu or tool is missing.It is in another workspace: switch with the tabs before the menus, or find it with Ctrl+K. Edit > Preferences > Settings... > Workspaces > All shows everything everywhere. In a narrow window, the menus are under ☰.
A menu row is greyed.Point at it: its tooltip says what it needs first (pick a limb, pick keys, open a scene).
A limb will not pick or will not move.Its track is locked. Right-click its name > Track > Lock track to unlock it.
The game plays it differently.Check Play as Roblox will, then the priority on Inspector > Clip: a higher priority clip on the same joints wins. Test bench > Game says which clip plays over it.
A limb jumps in one frame, or passes its key and comes back.Check animation lists each one at its frame with its fix. In Curves, Smooth gives the picked keys handles that never pass their keys.
Feet slide or float halfway through a blend.The clips' feet land at different points of their loops: see the ruler in Blend with, then Match steps.
Match steps says two keys would land on one frame.Delete one of the two keys it names, or make the clip longer (Key > Timing > Stretch timing...), then match again.
A Pose tool is greyed.An additive animation layer is picked: pose with the handles there, or key into Base (Key > Layers > Key into).
The character bounces after a punch.A whole-body squash keyed right after a move dips the body and brings it back. Key whole-body squash on landings; for a punch, stretch the arm.
An effect starts in the wrong place.Right-click its row > Place it > Where it hits, Where I click or At the limb's end. Or move its point with Studio's Move tool: the move is keyed at the playhead.
A change to an effect is not keyed.Changes made while Loop around it plays are not keyed. Choose Stop looping, go to the frame, then change the value.
The Camera preview shows only the picture.Clean view is on. Point at the window and click the eye in its top-right corner.
Clicking the other character does nothing.It joins the scene only once it has a row: Scene > Add > Rig > its name. A limb behind the posed character's limb goes to the one in front: click its row instead.
The camera jumps back when I move the playhead.Flying while looking through moves it until you press K; a frame change puts it back on its keys. Key it first.
The scene's sound is silent in Studio.The id must be a sound your account or group may use. Its row says "Loading the sound..." until Roblox gives it. Check the block's Volume on its Inspector page.
Put in the game says a clip is not published.With Motion set to Uploaded animations, every character's clip must be published. Publish it, or choose In the scene, which carries the motion in the scene itself.
The face changes in Framez but not in the game.The game needs FramezFace: Game > In the game > Face swaps in the game... > Add it to this game. Then publish the clip again.
There is no Face row.The head has no Decal and no FaceControls. Face > Face swaps > Give the head a face adds a face Decal. A Face row folded under Head opens with the arrow beside Head.
Exact lip sync is greyed or fails.It needs the Framez Helper 1.2 or newer: start it, or install it again from Help > Get the Framez Helper. The sound must be one this account may use.
A pasted decal id gives no face.Roblox would not open that decal for Framez. Paste the image's own id: in the Toolbox, insert the decal and copy the number from its Texture.
Framez says the Framez Helper is not running.Start "Framez Helper" from the Start menu, then click Retry. Not installed yet: install it. Image frames work without the helper.
"Studio stopped Framez from reaching the Framez Helper".Studio's question about 127.0.0.1 was answered no. Allow it: Plugins tab > Manage Plugins > Framez > HTTP requests.
"This place has HTTP requests turned off".Turn them on for the place: Experience Settings > Security > Allow HTTP Requests, then Retry.
Framez cannot add a script to the game.Allow it: Plugins tab > Manage Plugins > Framez > Script injection.
My videos are not in the clip list.Add their folder in Clip folders. Videos in folders inside it are not listed: add that folder too.
The folder picker did not come up.Paste the folder's path into the box under Add a clip folder... and press Enter.
Blender has no Framez tab.Edit > Preferences > Add-ons, search Framez, tick Framez Link. It needs Blender 4.2 or newer.
Joints are on the wrong person, or jump around.Zoom the Reference video window in until the person fills the white box.
Pose from video flipped a limb.Delete the bad keys and key them by hand against the Joints overlay, or run Key > Clean up > Euler filter.
Import says Roblox would not give the animation.Only your own, your groups' and Roblox's animations load. Import it from the place it lives in, or ask its owner.
An imported FBX clip has a limb wrong.Blender's Framez tab says which skeleton it read (Mixamo, R15, R6, or "named by words") and which joints it kept still. Rename unusual bones like Mixamo's (LeftArm, LeftForeArm, LeftUpLeg...) and import again.
Get from Blender says "Not on this rig".Those joints have other names in Blender. Rename the bones to the Motor6D names, or leave them out.
The animation is gone after reopening Studio.The place was not saved. Save in Framez, then save the place. While the Framez Helper runs every save is on disk too: drag its .rbxm (in the backup folder, by rig and name) into ServerStorage.RBX_ANIMSAVES and the rig's folder. Unsaved work also shows on the start screen: click Recover.
A change made it worse, and undo cannot reach back.File > History...: pick the version from before it.
The emote upload says "missing a valid UGC emote marker".It went up as a plain animation. In Emote..., click Save as emote, then step 1: Open Roblox's upload window, set Animation Type to Emote and tick Create Animation Object in Workspace. Then step 2 uploads the Animation it makes. It needs Studio 737 or later.
The emote upload says a body part is too far, too low or too fast.Open the clip and File > Export > Emote...: the same rule is listed at its frame. Pull the root back, or bend the knees where the body crouches.
Framez asks to give a rig Motor6Ds.The rig is joined with AnimationConstraints. Accept: each joint becomes a Motor6D between the same two attachments, and clips made on it play on either kind of rig. Undo puts the AnimationConstraints back.
