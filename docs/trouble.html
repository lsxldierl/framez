Troubleshooting

Each entry names something that looks wrong, says what usually causes it and how to put it right. The entries are grouped by where the problem shows up. Pressing Ctrl+K in Framez and typing a word also finds the tool that a fix names.

Menus, Keys and the Interface

A Menu or Tool Is Missing

The tool most likely belongs to another workspace. Switch workspaces with the tabs before the menus, or press Ctrl+K and search for the tool, because Menu Search lists the commands of every workspace and switches to the right one when you run it. To show every menu and tool in every workspace, set Menus and Tools to All in the Workspaces category of Preferences. In a narrow window the menus are folded into the ☰ button.

A Menu Row Is Greyed

A greyed row cannot run yet. Point at it, and its tooltip says what it needs first, such as selecting a limb, selecting keys or opening a scene.

A Keyboard Shortcut Does Nothing

Framez's keys work while a Framez window is active, so click in the Framez window first. A key also does nothing while a tool that is running owns it, for example while the slider is open, which is described under Fixed Keys. If you have changed keys, click By key in the Keyboard Shortcuts window and press the key to see which command has it now.

Studio's Own Undo Does Nothing While I Animate

While an animation is open, Framez switches Studio's undo off, and Ctrl+Z undoes Framez's own steps only. Close the animation to use Studio's undo again. If you delete the rig itself while its animation is open, Restore Deleted Rig brings it back.

A Limb Will Not Select or Will Not Move

Its track is locked. Right-click the limb's name in the track list, open the Track submenu and choose Lock track to unlock it.

Animation

The Game Plays the Animation Differently

Check Animator Preview, which plays the animation the way Roblox will. Then look at the priority on the Clip page of the Inspector, because an animation with a higher priority that plays on the same joints wins. The Test Bench in its Game view says which animation plays over yours.

A Limb Jumps in One Frame, or Passes Its Key and Comes Back

Check Animation lists each of these problems at its frame, with its fix. In the Curve Editor, setting the selected keys to Smooth gives them handles that never pass their keys.

Feet Slide or Float Halfway Through a Blend

The feet of the two animations land at different points of their loops. The ruler in Blend With shows where each animation's feet land, and Match Steps lines them up.

Match Steps Says Two Keys Would Land on One Frame

Two keys of one limb cannot share a frame, so Match Steps refuses to move them there and names the two keys. Delete one of them, or make the animation longer with Scale Keys, and then match again.

A Pose Tool Is Greyed

An additive animation layer is selected, and the pose tools do not work on it. Pose with the handles on that layer instead, or make Base the active layer from the Layers submenu of the Key menu.

The Character Bounces After a Punch

A whole-body squash that is keyed right after a move dips the body and then brings it back. Key a whole-body squash on landings, and for a punch stretch the arm instead. See Squash and Stretch.

Scenes and Effects

An Effect Starts in the Wrong Place

Right-click the effect's row, open Place it, and choose Where it hits, Where I click or At the limb's end. You can also move the effect's point with Studio's Move tool, and the move is keyed at the playhead. See Place.

A Change to an Effect Is Not Keyed

A change made while Loop Around It is playing is not keyed. Choose Stop looping, go to the frame, and then change the value.

The Camera Preview Shows Only the Picture

Clean view is on. Point at the window and click the eye in its top right corner to bring the controls back. See Clean View.

Clicking the Other Character Does Nothing

A character joins the scene only once it has a row in the scene, so add it from the Rig list under Add in the Scene menu. When one limb is behind another limb of the posed character, a click goes to the one in front. Click its row in the list instead.

The Camera Jumps Back When I Move the Playhead

Flying while looking through a camera moves it, but that movement is not keyed. Changing the frame puts the camera back on its keys. Press K to key the camera before you move the playhead.

The Scene's Sound Is Silent in Studio

The sound's id must be one that your account or group may use. Its row says "Loading the sound..." until Roblox provides it. Also check the Volume on the sound block's page in the Inspector.

Put in the Game Says an Animation Is Not Published

When Motion is set to Uploaded animations, every character's animation must be published first. Publish them, or set Motion to In the scene, which carries the motion inside the scene itself. See Put in the Game.

Faces

The Face Changes in Framez but Not in the Game

The game needs the FramezFace scripts. Choose Face swaps in the game... from the In the game submenu of the Game menu, and then choose Add it to this game. Publish the animation again afterwards. See Face Swaps in the Game.

There Is No Face Row

The head has neither a Decal nor FaceControls, so there is nothing to key. In the Face swaps submenu of the Face menu, Add face decal adds a face Decal. A Face row that is folded under Head opens with the arrow beside Head.

Exact Lip Sync Is Greyed or Fails

Exact lip sync needs version 1.2 or newer of the Framez Helper. Start the helper, or install it again with Get the Framez Helper. The sound must also be one that your account may use.

A Pasted Decal Id Gives No Face

Roblox would not open that decal for Framez. Paste the id of the image itself instead. Insert the decal from the Toolbox and copy the number from its Texture property.

The Helper, Video and Blender

Framez Says the Framez Helper Is Not Running

Start Framez Helper from the Start menu and click Retry. If it is not installed yet, install it. Image frames work without the helper.

"Studio Stopped Framez from Reaching the Framez Helper"

Studio asked whether Framez may connect to 127.0.0.1 and the answer was no. Allow it by opening Manage Plugins on the Plugins tab, selecting Framez, and allowing HTTP requests.

"This Place Has HTTP Requests Turned Off"

Turn them on for the place. Open the place's Experience Settings, go to Security, turn on Allow HTTP Requests, and click Retry in Framez.

Framez Cannot Add a Script to the Game

Studio has to allow it. Open Manage Plugins on the Plugins tab, select Framez, and allow Script injection.

My Videos Are Not in the Clip List

The helper reads only the folders it has been given. Add the folder that holds the videos in Clip Folders. Videos that sit in folders inside it are not listed, so add each of those folders as well.

The Folder Picker Did Not Come Up

Paste the folder's path into the box under Add a clip folder... and press Enter.

Blender Has No Framez Tab

The add-on is not switched on. Open Blender's Preferences, go to Add-ons, search for Framez and tick Framez Link. The add-on needs Blender 4.2 or newer.

Joints Are on the Wrong Person, or Jump Around

The joint tracking follows the part of the picture that lies inside the white box in the Reference video window. Zoom the window in until the person fills the white box.

Pose from Video Flipped a Limb

Delete the bad keys and key them by hand against the Joints overlay, or run Euler Filter on the animation to remove the flips.

An Imported FBX Animation Has a Limb Wrong

The Framez tab in Blender says which skeleton it read, such as Mixamo, R15, R6 or "named by words", and which joints it kept still. A bone with an unusual name may not have been recognised. Rename it to a standard name, for example the names Mixamo uses such as LeftArm, LeftForeArm and LeftUpLeg, and import again. See FBX Animation.

Get from Blender Says "Not on This Rig"

Those joints have different names in Blender than on the rig. Rename the bones in Blender to the Motor6D names, or leave them out. See Get from Blender.

Saving and Importing

The Animation Is Gone After Reopening Studio

The place was not saved after the animation was. Saving in Framez writes into the place, so save in Framez and then save the place. While the Framez Helper runs, every save is also written to disk. Drag its .rbxm file, which is in the backup folder under the rig's name, into the rig's folder under RBX_ANIMSAVES in ServerStorage. Unsaved work also shows on the start screen, where Recover brings it back. See Unsaved Work and Recovery.

Save Says There Is Nothing to Save

An animation with no keys is not saved. Key a pose first, and then save.

A Change Made It Worse, and Undo Cannot Reach Back

Open Version History and select the version from before the change.

Import Says Roblox Would Not Give the Animation

Roblox only provides your own animations, those of your groups, and Roblox's own. Import the animation from the place that it lives in, or ask its owner. See Roblox Animation.

Emotes and Rigs

The Emote Upload Says "missing a Valid UGC Emote Marker"

The animation was uploaded as a plain animation. Open Emote and click Save as emote. In step 1, open Roblox's upload window, set Animation Type to Emote and tick Create Animation Object in Workspace. Step 2 then uploads the Animation that step 1 made. The steps need Studio 0.737 or newer.

The Emote Upload Says a Body Part Is Too Far, Too Low or Too Fast

Open the animation and then Emote, where the same rule is listed at its frame. Pull the root back, or bend the knees where the body crouches.

Framez Asks to Give a Rig Motor6Ds

The rig is joined with AnimationConstraints, as Roblox builds a new R15. Accept the offer. Each joint becomes a Motor6D between the same two attachments, and animations made on it play on either kind of rig. Undo puts the AnimationConstraints back.

Create Joint Asks Me to Close the Animation First

The two parts belong to the rig that is open, and a rig's own parts cannot be joined or welded while its animation is open. Close the animation, make the joint, and open the animation again. To attach an object while you animate, use Hold the selected object from the right-click menu of a limb instead. See Create Joint and Hold.
